## Reading the Three Examples

**Example 1 (RP-001 Luxury Villa)** is a straightforward healthy case: Contract ₹1,50,00,000, Actual Expenses ₹42,00,000 so far, Forecast Profit ₹1,08,00,000, Margin 72% — early-stage project with most of the budget not yet spent, correctly classified `healthy`.

**Example 2 (RP-002 Commercial Complex)** demonstrates the Approved Variation impact this task's own Section 11 explicitly asks to test: Contract ₹2,50,00,000 alone would give a different Revenue Potential than the ₹2,65,00,000 shown — the ₹15,00,000 approved variation is genuinely included, not a static number. Margin (20.75%) is lower than Example 1's, reflecting a project further into its own cost curve, still correctly `healthy` since margin remains comfortably positive and no receivables are overdue.

**Example 3 (RP-003 Residential Tower)** is the deliberately adverse case: Actual Expenses (₹85,00,000) exceed Current Revenue Potential (₹80,00,000), producing a genuinely negative Forecast Profit (−₹5,00,000) and negative Margin (−6.25%). Combined with ₹24,00,000 in outstanding receivables against ₹24,00,000 billed (100% outstanding, 0% collection efficiency), this correctly classifies as `risk` with both `negative_forecast_margin` and `severely_overdue_receivables` — the exact two conditions this task's own Section 7 threshold table names for a Risk classification, both genuinely triggered by real underlying numbers, not asserted.

The client-safe version is the real output of `to_client_safe()` applied to the exact same internal report, not a separately-imagined example.

## The Difference, Made Explicit

Comparing the two outputs above directly:

- **Blockers & Risks:** the internal report includes `Owner: Procurement Coordinator` on the one open blocker; the client-safe report shows the identical blocker (same title, same age, same impact category) with the owner line removed entirely.
- **Commercial Attention:** the internal report has a full section ("Actual Cost Recorded"); the client-safe report has no Commercial Attention section at all — not an empty one, the section itself doesn't render.
- **Everything else is byte-for-byte identical:** Executive Summary, Work Completed Today, Site Activity Snapshot, Client Decisions Pending, AI Forecast Impact, and Attached Photo Summary are unchanged between the two — confirming the transformation is narrow and precise, not a blanket "internal vs. external" rewrite that risks hiding information a client should legitimately see (their own pending approval, the real photo count, the real forecast statement).
